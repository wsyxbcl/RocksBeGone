Settings
A group can hold hundreds of near-identical crops, so a few are shown that were picked to disagree with each other.

They are sampled in two halves. One half is taken straight off the top of the confidence ranking: an animal that keeps using the same trail scores high, so that is where a real one shows up. The other half is spread evenly across the different files in the group and across the run, because a rock's appearances differ only by the camera shifting or the light changing.

Show all displays every member of every group instead of a sample — the same as pressing “Show all” on each group in turn.

Toggle while reviewing with f.

For a video, the preview normally shows the clip's first frame — quick to decode, and enough to recognise the place.

Turn this on to decode the frame the detection actually came from. That is the one to look at when deciding whether a box is a rock or an animal, at the cost of decoding into the clip.
